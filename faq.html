Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to some common questions about SkyNet Co-Living Space. Please contact us if you need more information.

What is co-living?

Co-living is a style of accommodation where residents have their own private space while sharing selected common areas.

What types of packages are available?

SkyNet lists Premium, Standard, Short-Term and Custom package types. Contact us for current availability and details.

How can I find out the prices?

Prices may depend on the room, package and length of stay. Please contact us for an up-to-date quote.

Can I arrange a viewing?

Contact our team to ask whether an in-person or virtual viewing can be arranged.

What facilities are available?

Facilities can vary. Please check the Facilities page and contact us to confirm what is currently available.

Can I book a short stay?

A Short-Term package is listed. Contact us to confirm the available dates and minimum stay period.

Are utilities included in the package?

Utility arrangements depend on the package and property policy. Please confirm the details with us before booking.

How do I make an enquiry?

Use the Contact page to send an enquiry or reach us using the official contact details listed there.

Still have a question?

Send us a message and we'll help you find the information you need.
